Selected work

Project 01 · ENGR 122 · 2026

Robot Arena Autonomous navigation robot for the Gallois competition

Ranked 1st in preliminary standings and made the finals: 1 of 12 teams selected from roughly 650 first-year engineers.

Role
Led the housing design and printing. Helped debug pathfinding late in the build.
Tools
SolidWorks · FDM printing · Arduino · ArUco markers · Ultrasonic sensing
Timeline
Jan to May 2026
Result
1st in prelims · finalist, top 12 of ~650
Housing
Designed the main housing frame in SolidWorks to beat the spec on weight while staying stiff. I iterated the prints until it did both.
Pathfinding
Late in the project I stepped outside my usual scope to debug navigation. The robot combines camera-based ArUco marker localization for targeting checkpoints with ultrasonic feedback for avoiding obstacles.

Automatic Plant Watering System Soil-moisture sensing, automatic watering, wireless reporting

Reads soil moisture, waters on its own, and sends sensor readings over the Stevens wireless network.

Role

Tools
Arduino IDE · SolidWorks · FDM printing
Timeline
Sep to Dec 2025
Result
Working closed-loop watering system
Control
Arduino code reads the moisture sensor and drives the watering hardware, then transmits readings to the Stevens network.
Enclosure
Went through several revisions to cut print time and material while keeping it strong enough.

About

The long-term goal is engine design for supersonic flight. I'd rather build something, test it and fix it than leave it as a drawing, so I look for projects where I get my hands on the hardware.

I'm also secretary of our AIAA chapter and a member of ASME and NSBE.
