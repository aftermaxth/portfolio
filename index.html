Project 01 · ENGR 122 · 2026

Robot Arena Autonomous navigation robot for the Gallois competition

Ranked 1st in preliminary standings and made the finals: 1 of 12 teams selected from roughly 650 first-year engineers.

Role
Led the housing design and printing. Helped debug pathfinding late in the build.
Tools
SolidWorks · FDM printing · Arduino · ArUco markers · Ultrasonic sensing
Timeline
Jan to May 2026
Result
1st in prelims · finalist, top 12 of ~650 · 31 s competition run
Housing
Designed the main housing frame in SolidWorks around the print time limit without giving up strength. Circular lightening cutouts on the lateral panels cut material and time while keeping the frame rigid. The final slice came in at 2 hr 10 min against a 4 hour cap.
Pathfinding
Late in the project I stepped outside my usual scope to debug navigation. The robot combines camera-based ArUco marker localization for targeting checkpoints with ultrasonic feedback for avoiding obstacles.

Project 02 · ENGR 111 · 2025

Automatic Plant Watering System Soil-moisture sensing, automatic watering, wireless reporting

Reads soil moisture, waters on its own, and sends sensor readings over the Stevens wireless network.

Role
Led the SolidWorks design and the Arduino programming.
Tools
Arduino IDE · SolidWorks · FDM printing
Timeline
Sep to Dec 2025
Result
Deployed 5 days · telemetry every 10 min
Control
Waters 20 mL every 24 hours, and any time soil moisture reads under 30%. Readings go to the Stevens network every 10 minutes. The DHT11 and the soil sensor each worked alone but neither worked with both on the breadboard, and nothing threw an error. An hour of wiring permutations later, the answer was that both had to share one power supply and one ground line.
Enclosure
Three revisions. The first did not fit the 5 x 5 x 10 inch envelope, the second fit but left no room for the components, and the final one dropped the bottle into a slide-in slot at the base and gave every board its own slot on a rear shelf. Cutouts in the dividing wall route the wiring and cut print time. Final slice: 6 hr 51 min against an 8 hour cap.

Project 03 · Software · Personal

Training Hub V2 Coaching web app for the athletes I write training for

I coach, the athletes log. Tracks weekly mileage and workouts, plus goals sorted into Safe, Solid and Reach tiers with a weekly process tracker.

Role
Solo, AI-assisted build. I designed the data model and permissions on paper before any code existed.
Tools
Git · GitHub · HTML · CSS · JavaScript · Supabase · Claude
Timeline
Three days, September 2026
Result
Three days from never having installed Git to a working app in real use
Data model
Supabase and Postgres behind a vanilla HTML, CSS and JS front end, no framework. Tables for athletes, weeks, sessions, resets, goals, races and lift cycles, scoped so the schema holds a full roster rather than one athlete.
Permissions
The database has no concept of a coach. It sees two accounts that both logged in, and two separate problems: scoping who can see which rows, and stopping an athlete from editing their own prescribed workout instead of just logging it. Neither one throws an error.
